Enter the patient's home address to see whether it is in an RHTP rural area. CommHIT Everywhere is part of Florida's Rural Health Transformation Program.

Street, city and ZIP code. Do not enter a name or date of birth.

About this check

Privacy. This page stores nothing and sends nothing to CommHIT. When the ZIP code alone can't decide, only the street address is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public address lookup to find its census block.

Rules. AHCA RHTP Definition of Rural (March 2026), as applied by CommHIT with AHCA's November 7, 2026 decisions. Sources: FloridaCommerce REDI list (effective March 5, 2026); HRSA FORHP rural data (September 2025); USDA 2020 RUCA codes; 2020 Census. Not an official AHCA determination.
